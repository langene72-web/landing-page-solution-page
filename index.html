The Operational Trust Assessment

Fix the workflow before you automate it.

Operational trust means confidence that the work gets done correctly without you checking it. The Operational Trust Assessment finds exactly where that confidence breaks down in your practice and what to do about it, in order of impact.

  • Simplify, then automate

    Clear ownership and simpler handoffs solve most problems. AI and automation come in only where they clearly remove effort or error.

Questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. It's a 15-minute conversation about what's slowing your practice down, with no cost and no obligation. The goal is that you leave with a clearer sense of where to look first.

We're a small practice. Is this too much for us?

No. This is built for independent and small practices. Smaller teams often feel fragile workflows the most, because one absence affects everything. The assessment is sized to your practice, not to a hospital system.

Will AI replace my staff?

No. The goal is to take repetitive checking and chasing off your team's plate, so they can focus on patients and on the work that needs a person. Many of the fixes don't involve AI at all.

Do you sell software?

No. I'm vendor-neutral: I don't sell or resell software, and I don't take commissions. If a tool makes sense, I'll help you evaluate your options on your terms.

Do I need to share patient information?

No. The consultation doesn't require any patient information. We talk about how work flows through your practice, not about individual patients.
